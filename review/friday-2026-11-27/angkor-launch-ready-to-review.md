## English
Ultra-Trail d'Angkor is now on Bibly.

On Saturday 23 January 2027 the 10th edition runs through Angkor Archaeological Park on dirt tracks, sandy paths and rice-field lanes, past Ta Som, Phnom Bok, Banteay Samre, Pre Rup and Ta Nei, and finishes at the Terrace of the Elephants in Angkor Thom.

Distances from 4 km to 100 km, including the UTA 100, Bayon Trail 64 km, Marathon Trail 42 km, Jungle Trail 32 km and Naga Trail 21.1 km, plus untimed Walk & Hike routes.

Entries close 10 January. Link in bio.
Organised by SDPO. Photos: SDPO / Ultra-Trail d'Angkor.
